Shotlink guides / Expiring links

Share an expiring link with a clear deadline

An expiring route helps end a short sharing window. It stops the Shotlink from redirecting after a deadline; it does not revoke access to the original destination.

Create a free workspace →Try a temporary link

Guest: up to 30 minutes. Workspace: up to 7 days. No payment details needed for Free.

How to use it

  1. Decide what must expire

    If only the short route should stop working, choose a Shotlink expiry. If the document or form must become inaccessible, also change access at the destination.

  2. Choose guest or workspace sharing

    Guest links offer 5, 10, 15, or 30 minutes without an account. Verified workspaces offer link lifetimes up to seven days and workspace click analytics.

  3. Share the deadline

    Tell recipients when the route closes, including the time zone when relevant. A clear deadline prevents a broken-link surprise.

  4. Check the end of the window

    After expiry, the short route shows an expired-link response. The QR uses that same route and stops redirecting too. Independently close or restrict the destination if required.

A practical example

To share an agenda during a 30-minute discussion, use a guest link and state the closing time. If someone saves the original agenda URL, they may still open it later; restrict the agenda itself if access must end.

Before you share

  • Expiry is not password protection and does not erase copies or downloads.
  • A fallback can help an active route when its primary destination is unavailable. It does not extend an expired link's lifetime.
  • Workspace links are not permanent: the current maximum lifetime is seven days.
  • Do not use a temporary route for account recovery links or other content that must remain reachable beyond its expiry.

Common questions

Does expiry delete the destination?

No. Shotlink stops routing. The destination remains under its owner's control.

Will a fallback open after expiry?

No. Expiry closes the short route. Configure destination access and your communication plan separately.
